FIG.08 — The paper

Six documents you read
before I build anything.

"The plan before the pixels" is not a mood. It is six short documents, in this order, each one written in plain language and each one yours to keep whether or not you go ahead. You sign the last three before money moves.

  1. 01

    The requirements

    What it does, feature by feature, and a list of what is not in this version. If a feature is not on the page it is not in the build.

    You read: a features table, and what is excluded

  2. 02

    The technical sheet

    The tools, decided once and written down, each with a one-line reason in plain English. So nobody is guessing halfway through.

    You read: what it is built on, and why

  3. 03

    The app flow

    Every page a person can reach, what each button does, which page comes next, and where they land when something goes wrong.

    You read: no dead ends

  4. 04

    The design brief

    Your real colours and fonts, and the rules for buttons, forms, headings and tone — written so the tenth screen looks as on-brand as the first.

    You read: your colours, not ones that look close

  5. 05

    The data sheet

    What is stored, how records link, and exactly who can see, change and delete each one — with anything personal or sensitive flagged.

    You read: who can see what

  6. 06

    The build order

    The stages, what you get at each one, how you test it yourself, and what you sign off before the next stage starts. Riskiest first.

    You read: a sign-off you can do alone
