## 2. The six fixes, ranked by what they cost you

### 1. Add the website link — still missing after four attempts

**Edit profile → Links → Add link**
- URL `https://hotmoon123.github.io/hotmoon123/`
- Title `Packs, prices & how to order`
- **Show as primary link: ON**

The setup checklist ticked “Complete your profile” anyway. That is why this keeps getting missed.

**Why it is first:** every Reel view, every profile visit, every flyer scan currently dead-ends here. Nothing else you do matters as much until this exists.

### 2. Remove the generated-woman post from the grid

Not because it's ugly — because the first thing a stranger sees is a woman who does not exist, holding a pack. If one customer recognises that and says so publicly, the trust cost is permanent and it spreads.

It also still carries the rejected slogan.

**Fix:** open the post → ⋯ → **Archive**. Do not delete (archive keeps the insights and is reversible).

**Grid order should become:** the hand-pickup Reel → the Premium poster → next new video.

### 3. Use “Invite friends” while the page is small

This is the one built-in growth lever for a 4-follower account, and it is already on your screen.

**Settings and activity → How others can interact with you → Follow and invite friends → Invite**

Invite your contact list by WhatsApp — the same people who already order from you. Target: **25 invitations from people who have bought before**. A cold account cannot be grown by content alone; the first fifty followers come from people who already know the food.

### 4. Stop posting stills as Reels

A poster inside a 9:16 frame with black space above and below reads as “no effort” to both viewers and ranking. The hand-pickup clip is the standard to hold: full frame, food close, hand moving, real sound.

**Rule:** every Reel has motion in the first second, fills the frame, and runs 6–15 seconds.

### 5. Add banners

`Add banners` is free real estate directly under the bio — the most-viewed area of the page.

**Use it for:** one banner with the three packs and prices, one with “WhatsApp 0595 638 696 to order”. No QR code — nobody scans from a phone they are using.

### 6. Set the safety switches now, while the page is quiet

- **Hidden words** → add `scam`, `fake`, `thief`, `poison` (brand-protection, not censorship)
- **Limit interactions** → leave **Off** during trading hours; use **On** only during a pile-on
- **Comment filters** → keep Instagram's default filter ON

## 6. Governance for this account

- **AI-generated people must not appear as customers.** Product illustration is defensible; a synthetic person holding the pack is not. Label any such image plainly if it is ever reused, or don't use it.
- **Consent before filming customers.** Verbal permission on camera is enough; keep it simple and never film a child without a parent present and agreeing.
- **Music** — use Instagram's in-app audio library only. Audio pulled from other apps is a rights problem and can mute the post.
- **WhatsApp and DM data** fall under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843): collect only what you need to deliver, don't forward customer numbers, and don't add people to broadcast lists without asking.
- **No invented reviews, follower counts, or “sold out” urgency.** Advertise what is in the box and what it costs.
- **Disclose paid partnerships** with `#ad` in the first two lines if a creator is ever paid or given a free pack in exchange for posts.
