## 4. Provisional expansion plan — grow demand before overhead

The best next move is not automatically a shop, paid ads or a large production increase. Use the two channels already selling first, measure what happens, then test a small number of nearby customer groups.

### First 30 days (proposed; adjust to capacity)

**Week 1 — Get the offer and baseline straight**
- Confirm product varieties, exact pack mix, package condition, current prices and included drinks.
- Record recent orders by channel (church/home), pack/product state, quantity, price, payment, actual inputs, waste and fulfilment result.
- Work out actual variable cost per batch and per order, including ingredients, cooking oil/energy, drinks, packaging and order-specific costs. Leave unknown values blank until measured.

**Week 2 — Make ordering repeatable**
- Set realistic order deadlines and collection times; confirm whether pickup/delivery is offered before advertising it.
- Use WhatsApp Business if the owner wants it and confirms an active number: accurate catalogue, separate fried/unfried/frozen listings, saved replies and order-status labels.
- Keep a separate order-and-cash record; get permission before sending repeat marketing or publishing customer names, photographs or reviews.

**Week 3 — Test two expansion routes**
- First, invite existing church/home customers to pre-order and refer interested people, with church permission for any group/channel promotion.
- Second, test one nearby customer group—e.g. a small office/workplace or event organiser—using a limited trial matched to proven capacity. Ask permission to send the menu; agree quantity, price, payment, deadline and handover before accepting.
- Do not make speculative stock or promise recurring supply before checking costs and capacity.

**Week 4 — Review, then decide**
- Compare enquiries, confirmed orders, completed orders, repeat buyers, contribution by product/pack, waste and on-time handovers by channel.
- Repeat only the channel/offer that produced useful customer evidence and positive contribution. Consider equipment, paid marketing, reseller deals or premises only after evidence of a real constraint and an affordable case.

Suggested simple funnel: **share accurate offer → enquiry → qualify product/state/quantity/timing/location → confirm total and handover → record → fulfil → invite feedback**.

## 5. Simple operating and money controls

For each order record: date/reference; customer source; product; state (fried/unfried/frozen); exact samosa/spring-roll mix; quantity; quoted and confirmed amount; drink/packaging; payment status; agreed handover; actual variable costs; waste/issue/refund; completion and feedback. Limit personal customer data, obtain permission for marketing follow-up, and keep the records access-controlled.

At batch level record ingredient quantities/prices, saleable yield, cooking inputs, packaging and waste. Calculate contribution only from actual data:

**Contribution per order = amount received − actual variable costs attributable to that order.**

Contribution is not net profit; account separately for recurring overheads and owner withdrawals. Do not set numerical growth targets until a baseline exists.

For frozen and unfried items, safe handling/storage and truthful customer instructions are prerequisites. Confirm the actual process and relevant Ghanaian requirements with competent local authorities before expanding packaging, storage or distribution. No regulatory research or compliance determination has been completed here.
