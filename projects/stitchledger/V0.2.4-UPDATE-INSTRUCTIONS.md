# StitchLedger v0.2.4 update — mobile form clarity and modal-error fix

This combined update includes all v0.2.1–v0.2.3 work plus the v0.2.4 payment/cancellation mobile usability remediation.

## What changes

- Payment/refund **Reference / reason** is visibly required.
- The payment/refund confirmation button remains unavailable until amount and reference/reason are entered.
- The app gives a plain-language instruction rather than showing normal raw backend validation text for an empty reason.
- A stale error is cleared before cancellation confirmation, avoiding duplicate background/modal warning messages.
- The backend database, order/payment rules and existing records are not changed.
