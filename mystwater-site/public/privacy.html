Privacy notice

This is a concept website prepared for MYST WATER COMPANY by Caleb Bentum. It exists to be evaluated by the company; it is not an official business website.

What this site collects

Nothing by itself. There are no analytics, no cookies, no advertising trackers and no accounts. WhatsApp links open WhatsApp on your device; telephone links open your dialer; email links open your mail client. Any message you send is handled by WhatsApp, your phone network or your email provider respectively.

The wholesale enquiry form

The stockist form does not silently submit data anywhere. Choosing “Send enquiry on WhatsApp” opens WhatsApp with your details pre-filled so you can send the message yourself. Choosing “Send by email” opens your mail client with your details pre-filled to info@drinkmyst.com. No information is stored on this site or on its host.

Google Maps embed

The contact section embeds Google Maps to show the listed location. If you load it, Google may receive your IP address and usage data under Google’s privacy policy.

Your rights (Ghana)

The Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) gives you the right to be informed about how your personal data is used, to access it, to correct it and to request deletion. Because this site stores nothing, there is minimal data to exercise those rights over; for any data the business itself holds, write to info@drinkmyst.com.

Marks and imagery

“MYST”, “MYST WATER”, “The Feel Good Water”, product imagery and campaign artwork are the property of MYST WATER COMPANY / PNF Industries Limited. They are shown here for evaluation purposes only. Prices, availability, hours and order details are confirmed directly with the business and are intentionally not hard-coded here.
