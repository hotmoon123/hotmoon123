# BiteKasa — screen recording script

**Not yet recorded.** This is the shot list for a single take. The recording has to be made on a
device with a browser; it cannot be produced from the repository.

Two versions: a **90-second desktop take** for portfolios, and a **60-second phone take** for
social. Record one, not both, until the first is good.

---

## Before you press record

- Screen recording at **1080p**, 30 fps is enough.
- **Turn on Do Not Disturb.** One notification in frame ruins the take.
- Close every tab except the site. No bookmarks bar.
- Use a **clean browser profile** — no extensions visible, no personal tabs.
- Decide the order of clicks **before** recording. Hesitation reads as doubt.
- Record in **one take**. Cut nothing. A continuous take is more convincing than a polished edit.
- Show the **URL bar** in frame. Proof is half the point.

Free tools: Android screen recorder, iOS Control Centre → Screen Recording, or OBS on desktop
(free, records the browser window only).

## Desktop take — 90 seconds

| Time | Action | What it proves | Caption to add |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:06 | Show URL bar, press Enter, page loads | It is live and fast on a cold load | "Live on free hosting" |
| 0:06–0:16 | Scroll the hero into the three pack cards | Prices visible without hunting | "Three prices, first screen" |
| 0:16–0:26 | Hover a card slowly (2 px lift), then move on | Motion confirms, does not perform | "Hover: 2 px lift, nothing more" |
| 0:26–0:40 | Tap **+** twice on Silver, once on Golden; watch the total re-key | The order builder works with no account | "Builds an order, no login" |
| 0:40–0:50 | Press **Send order on WhatsApp**, show the pre-filled message | The handoff is real and readable | "One tap opens WhatsApp, pre-written" |
| 0:50–1:00 | Back out **without sending** | Nothing is stored or submitted silently | "Nothing is sent until the customer sends it" |
| 1:00–1:10 | Open `/services/fried/` and `/privacy.html` | More than one page; privacy is stated | "Static pages, indexable" |
| 1:10–1:20 | Open devtools → device toolbar → phone width, reload | Responsive, not a squeezed desktop | "Built for a phone first" |
| 1:20–1:30 | Hold on the three packs, stop | End on the product | "BiteKasa — samosas & spring rolls" |

Total: 90 seconds. If you overrun, cut 1:00–1:10 first.

## What NOT to record

- **Your Instagram analytics.** Four followers in frame undercuts everything else you show.
  Record the product, not the account, until the account has something worth showing.
- **Fake orders or invented messages.** Do not stage a WhatsApp conversation you did not have.
- **A section that is not on the live site.** If something is still staged, it does not exist yet.
- **Your desktop clutter.** No personal folders, no email client, no other tabs.

---

## After the take

- Name the file `bitekasa-site-<yyyy-mm-dd>-<desktop|phone>.mp4`.
- Keep the **original, unedited** file. Edits are for sharing; the raw take is the evidence.
- Store it beside this script, and link it from `CASE_STUDY.md` §4 once it exists.
- If you upload to Instagram or a portfolio, **say what is being shown**: a live site, measured
  budgets, no sales claims.
